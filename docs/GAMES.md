# Snowpack: Play (study games), Phase 11

Not part of Phases 6-10. Build after the core app works. Ideas that were judged bad for learning have been removed.

## Rules for every game
- **Games are practice. They never move FSRS** (speed pressure and guessing would distort it).
- **Misses are logged:** a missed card is flagged weak and reappears more often in later rounds.
- **Games use the same scope selection as every mode:** a course, or the chosen topics and sections.
- **Controls:** one tap, one swipe or one drag. Nothing needs a keyboard.
- **Visual timers** (a shrinking bar or ring), not numbers.
- **Juice:** shake, a brief colour flash, particles and short sounds. Reduced-motion safe; sounds on their own toggle.
- **Stars and scores reward accuracy first;** speed only as a tiebreak.
- **No streak guilt, no expected-reward chests, no pressure tricks.**

## Build first
| Game | How it plays | Learning value |
|---|---|---|
| **Match** | A 3×4 grid of terms and definitions (an even number of tiles), with a timer. A wrong pair shakes, flashes a colour, stays, and adds time. A right pair turns green and disappears | Recognition, fast and proven (Quizlet) |
| **Constellation** (a skin of Match) | Glowing stars on a night sky; drag lines between matching pairs | Same as Match, with a Snowpack night look |
| **Gate Dash** | Yuki runs automatically; the term is at the top; swipe lanes into the gate with the right answer | Multiple choice under light pressure |
| **Pack Sort** | Terms fall like snowflakes; swipe each into its category den (prevent/detect/respond/recover, OSI layers, malware types) | Category discrimination, the highest learning value here |
| **Ice Bridge** | Drag ice blocks into the right order to cross the river | Sequences (layers, process steps) |
| **60-second Blitz** | As many questions as possible in 60 s; 5 right in a row starts Fever mode (10 s, points doubled, a glow) | Mixed retrieval, replayable |
| **Sudden Death** (a variant of Blitz) | 1 life, no timer; every 10 correct speeds up the animations and raises the points; one mistake ends the run | Streak challenge |

## Later
| Game | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Jeopardy** | Topics × 100-500 points; answer by choice; a Daily Double. Best as 2 players passing one phone |
| **Odd Wolf Out** | 4 items; tap the one that doesn't belong |
| **Cheat-Table Fill** | The course's @cheat table with blanks; drag the values in |

## Mechanics to use
- Combo multipliers (3+ right in a row → 2x, 3x).
- An overdrive finish (faster music and double points in the last 10 s). Games only, never reading.
- Power-ups earned from right answers: **50/50** and **Freeze Time** (5 s).
- Smart repetition: missed cards return more often in the next round.
- Personal bests per game and course.
- Occasional surprise cosmetic gifts (a theme colour, a Yuki accessory). Never tied to streaks and never expected.

## The progression game: "Snowpack Expedition" (after Play is built)
- **Map:** each course is a mountain range, each topic a region, each section a camp.
- **Campfires:** each camp's fire glows with the real memory strength of its cards (FSRS retrievability). It fades as you forget and is rekindled by reviewing. Progress reflects actual memory, never guilt.
- **Rewards:** mastering a topic earns a new pack member (a wolf) or den decorations, all cosmetic.
- **First step:** a simple map look for the topic list. The full expedition comes later.
